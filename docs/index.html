ABOUT · MICHAEL SCHULTZ

From space to
better decisions.

Earth observation, open geospatial data, and practical tools for research and decision-making.

I’m Michael Schultz, an earth observation researcher based in Tübingen, Germany. I am currently Acting Professor of Remote Sensing at Friedrich Schiller University Jena, with academic affiliations at the University of Tübingen and the University of Jena.

I connect satellite observations, machine learning, and open data with questions about land use, climate, infrastructure, and environmental change.
