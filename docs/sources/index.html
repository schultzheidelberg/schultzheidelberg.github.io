Past-work figures & experience

The Vietnam carbon map is Figure 2.5 in Michael Schultz’s doctoral thesis (2018), Chapter 2, from the study by Avitabile et al. (2016). The vegetation-index figures are the spider plots in Figure 4.7 and the bar plots in Figure 4.3, Chapter 4, from Schultz et al. (2016). The spider plots show relative performance ranks across six criteria; positions farther from the centre indicate better performance. These previews reproduce the figures without changing their data; surrounding thesis text is omitted.

The KAZA map is Figure 1 from Schultz et al. (2018), reused under CC BY 4.0 and resized proportionally. All previews link to their full-size figures or source studies.
