FLOOD RESILIENCE · HUẾ, VIETNAM

FRAME & FloodAdaptVN

How can flood-risk research become something people can explore and use? My work on FRAME connects geospatial data, flood scenarios, and accessible web tools for Huế and Central Vietnam.

The platform is part of FloodAdaptVN, a German–Vietnamese collaboration linking flood-risk research with adaptation planning. Its current implementation phase runs from June 2025 to May 2027.

LAND MONITORING · UKRAINE · 2025–PRESENT

PAABS: mapping land for climate reporting

Since October 2025, I have contributed to the PAABS programme’s land-monitoring work in Ukraine. In 2026, I coordinated land-use and land-cover classification combining Sentinel-2 observations, OpenStreetMap-derived training data, and reproducible processing workflows.

The work supports Ukraine’s Land Parcel Identification System and land-use, land-use-change and forestry reporting. It connects detailed satellite mapping with the information needed for climate policy.

EARTH OBSERVATION · AFRICA · RESEARCH PREVIEW

Border Observatory

Move from an Africa-wide overview to individual border crossings, then explore how estimated occupied area varies across the observation archive. This interactive viewer connects map features with historical satellite-derived summaries.

The indicator measures estimated occupied area; it does not directly measure vehicle counts, queues, or crossing times.

LAND USE · HEIDELBERG COLLABORATION

OSMlanduse

Explore OpenStreetMap’s land-use and land-cover information through the interactive OSM Landuse Landcover platform. I co-led the project with Alexander Zipf, working with colleagues at Heidelberg University and HeiGIT.

My contribution includes the deep-learning methods for filling gaps in OpenStreetMap coverage using Sentinel-2 imagery, developed with Hao Li and Zhaoyan Wu. The website was developed by Michael Auer and Moritz Schott; the platform’s “Info & Contact” section credits the wider team.

The associated 10-metre European land-use dataset combines OpenStreetMap and Sentinel-2, connecting volunteered geographic information with satellite observations and deep learning.

2020 reference year · Published in Scientific Data, 2025 · Joint first authors: Michael Schultz and Hao Li
